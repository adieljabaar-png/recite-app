Sunnah Salaah — Imam Shafi'i

SUNNAH BEFORE FARD

2 Rak'ahs • Sunnah Before • Mu'akkadah

Pray 2 rak'ahs before Fard Fajr. Emphasised Sunnah.

SUNNAH AFTER FARD

None for Fajr in SHAFI'I.

Mu'akkadah = emphasised Sunnah, strongly encouraged. Ghayr Mu'akkadah = optional Sunnah. Nafl = extra voluntary prayer. Witr in SHAFI'I: Sunnah, 1 or 3 rak'ahs.

Standing — Qiyam

Niyyah — Intention

In both madhabs the Niyyah is in the HEART. The Arabic wording below is a teaching aid to help you know what you are intending - you do not have to say it aloud to make your Salaah valid.

ARABIC — NIYYAH

نَوَيْتُ أَنْ أُصَلِّيَ فَرْضَ الْفَجْرِ رَكْعَتَيْنِ مُسْتَقْبِلَ الْقِبْلَةِ أَدَاءً لِلَّهِ تَعَالَى

TRANSLITERATION — SAY / KNOW

Nawaytu an usalliya fardhal-fajri rak'ataini mustaqbilal-qiblati adaa'an lillahi ta'ala

TRANSLATION — ENGLISH

I intend to pray 2 rak'ahs of the obligatory Fajr prayer, facing the Qiblah, on time, for Allah Most High.

Behind an Imam? If praying behind an Imam, add: muqtadiyan bihadhā al-imām / following this Imam. If praying alone, leave that part out.

WHAT TO DO

Stand facing the qiblah, calm and still. Make the intention in your heart for: Fajr Fard, 2 rak'ahs, for Allah. The Arabic wording below is a teaching aid — you do not have to say it aloud.
