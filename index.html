🤲 Duah of the Day

رَبَّنَا إِنَّنَا آمَنَّا فَاغْفِرْ لَنَا ذُنُوبَنَا وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Our Lord, indeed we have believed, so forgive us our sins and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.

Surah Ali 'Imran 3:16

📚 Tafsir of the Duah

The declaration of the people of understanding who affirm their faith and ask for forgiveness of sins.

📜 Hadith of the Day

لاَ يُؤْمِنُ أَحَدُكُمْ حَتَّى يُحِبَّ لأَخِيهِ مَا يُحِبُّ لِنَفْسِهِ

None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself.

Sahih al-Bukhari 13 · Sahih Muslim 45

💡 Explanation of the Hadith

This hadith defines the social dimension of faith. It is not enough to worship Allah in isolation; true belief requires wanting good for others as you want for yourself. Envy, greed and selfishness are diseases of the heart that contradict faith. When a believer truly loves for their brother what they love for themselves, they will not cheat, oppress or wish harm — they will instead help, support and celebrate others' success. The Prophet ﷺ made this a measure of sincerity: whoever does not wish good for others has not yet tasted the sweetness of faith.
